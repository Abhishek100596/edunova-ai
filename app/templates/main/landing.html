FAQ

Questions, answered

Is the placement probability a guarantee?

No. EDUNOVA estimates readiness from your profile signals. Outcomes depend on market conditions, interviews, and effort. Always treat scores as guidance.

Do I need to pay to start?

You can create an account and begin onboarding for free. Premium AI features may depend on your campus or plan configuration.

Is my data secure?

Profiles and uploads stay in your EDUNOVA deployment. Never share secrets in chat. Admins only see what your institution configures.

What appears as Demo Preview?

Landing and empty-state illustrations may show labelled Demo Preview values so you can see the UI before your real data is ready.
